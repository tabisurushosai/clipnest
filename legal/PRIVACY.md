# Clipnest Privacy Policy

Clipnest stores clipboard history and settings locally in `chrome.storage.local` on your device.

## Data stored locally

- Clipboard clips, tags, templates, and settings remain on your device.
- Clipnest does not sell clipboard data or use it for advertising.

## External communication

Clipnest sends data outside your device only in these cases:

1. Premium users who enable AI features send clipboard text to Google Gemini using the user's own API key.
2. License verification sends `license_key` and `extension_id` to `/api/verify-license`.

## Data deletion

You can delete all local data from the options page using **Reset all data**.
